The Practice — Healing, Alignment, and Awakening Sensitivity
Written April 2026 · Living Document
This document is the living foundation from which The Practice grows. It is not The Practice itself — it is the soul behind it. Read this before reading The Practice. Return to it whenever The Practice starts to feel like something other than what it is.

1. The Core

Everything in this practice is in service of this:

The Five Orientations
  • Alignment
  • Healthy sensitivity attunement
  • Love
  • Being of service
  • Raising to high vibrational frequency

This is not a set of goals to achieve in sequence. It is a way of being that the healing journey is moving toward — and that the journey itself, when inhabited rightly, already expresses.

The condition that initiated this inquiry is not the destination. The practice is the container that supports the becoming that the condition was always pointing toward.

This core is the compass. When any part of The Practice feels wrong — too heavy, too mechanical, too driven by urgency — return to these five orientations and ask: is what I am doing genuinely in service of this?

2. The Condition as Messenger

The sound sensitivity condition that has significantly affected daily life — shared spaces, shared meals, ordinary intimacy with others — is real, sometimes painful, and for a long time has felt like something to be defeated.

It is not something to be defeated.

It is a messenger. One that has been pointing — insistently, sometimes loudly, sometimes at great personal cost — toward something that needed attention. Not just a nervous system requiring calibration. A sensitivity that needed to be understood, honored, and brought into alignment with its true purpose.

The hypersensitivity that makes certain sounds overwhelming is the same sensitivity that, developed and aligned, becomes the capacity for deep attunement — to people, to subtle energy, to guidance, to the living intelligence that moves through all things.

The healing is not the silencing of that signal.
It is the honoring of what it was pointing at.

When that alignment deepens, the signal naturally quiets — because it has been heard.

There will be moments when this larger perspective is not accessible — when the condition is simply painful and the elevated view feels very far away. The practice holds space for those moments too. The messenger does not require gratitude at every moment. It only asks that, over time, it be listened to.

3. The Governing Philosophy

The healing intelligence is already present.

This practice does not create healing. It creates the conditions in which healing that is already seeking to happen can move through. The nervous system knows how to restore itself. The sensitivity knows what it is for. The body knows the way home.

The work is to stop interfering with that restoration and to actively support the conditions it needs.

This is not passive. Creating genuine conditions requires real effort, real discipline, real courage, and real love. But the orientation is fundamentally different from a plan designed to defeat a condition.

This is not war. This is restoration.

The question this practice is organized around is not: how do we reduce reactivity?

It is: what restores alignment and develops the capacity to hold sensitivity as a gift rather than a wound?

Symptom reduction follows from this. It is not the goal. It is the natural byproduct of genuine alignment. In a practice organized around alignment, nothing is wasted. Difficult weeks are information. Slow periods are incubation. Everything is material for the journey.

4. The Three Living Movements

This is not a ladder to be climbed. It is a spiral — three movements that breathe throughout the entire journey, each always present, each more emphasized at different moments. A single session can move through all three. A difficult week may call for return to the first. The arc is real but it is not linear. It is alive.

I
Descent & Restoration
Go underground before acting. Protected withdrawal into the root layer. Quieting the noise of the surface world enough that deeper signals can be heard. This is not preparation for healing — it is the beginning of it.
II
Alignment & Attunement
When genuine stillness is produced, something reorients. Not through effort — through the quiet the descent created. The overflowing source begins feeding the whole vertical system naturally. The heart of the practice.
III
Embodiment & Service
What has been developed is brought back into daily life. The sensitivity now oriented outward as gift rather than inward as wound. This movement is ongoing. It is what the practice was always for.

These three movements do not replace each other. They deepen together. They breathe throughout the entire journey. The community — even as the primary trigger environment — is simultaneously the primary field of practice for the third movement.

5. The Listening Pier

The most important structural element of this entire practice.

Developing felt-sense, deep listening, intuitive attunement, energy reading, and conscious connection with guidance beyond the physical is not one practice among many. It is the foundational skill that everything else serves and from which everything else takes its proper direction.

The sensitive faculty that the condition has been expressing in its dysregulated form is the same faculty being consciously developed here. The healing and the awakening are the same movement, traveling in the same direction.

The science and the spirit are not in conflict here. Interoception is the same neural pathway that behavioral rewriting works through. Dreams are memory consolidation. The theta-alpha state of deep meditation is precisely where unconscious material surfaces. Synchronicities are pattern recognition from a quieted mind. And plant medicine encounters are genuine dialogues with an intelligence that communicates.

The Trust Hierarchy
  1. 1Direct felt sense and deep listening in the present moment
  2. 2What emerged from plant medicine sessions and their integration
  3. 3Dreams, synchronicities, and received inspiration and guidance
  4. 4Trusted guides, practitioners, and community wisdom
  5. 5The practice structure and neuroscience framework
  6. 6External tools and support structures
The decision-making principle: Before any significant decision in this practice, the first question is not "what does the structure say?" — it is "what does deep listening say?" External sources inform. Internal sources govern.

6. Vertical Alignment — The Primary Work

One overflowing source feeds the whole vertical system. The energy centers of the body are not separate systems to be managed independently. They are a single column. When the vertical connection is restored, the whole system reorganizes.

The organizing question of this practice is not: which intervention addresses which symptom?

It is: what restores and deepens vertical flow?

The criterion is not mechanistic efficiency alone. It is: does this restore and deepen the column?

What Restores the Column
  • Sleep — not consolidation substrate, the primary alignment practice. The nightly descent where the column is most naturally permeable.
  • Nourishment — food as an act of care for the instrument. The physical foundation of the column's clarity.
  • Embodied movement and somatic practice — releasing chronic tension, restoring the body as an open channel.
  • Movement in nature — physical practice in natural environment restores felt vertical aliveness in a way indoor practice cannot replicate.
  • Deep meditative practice — accessing the threshold state where the column is naturally more permeable.
  • Plant medicine — the full column becomes accessible. An encounter with intelligence that shows what genuine vertical openness feels like.
  • Heart-opening work — the layer where love was replaced by fear. Where the interpersonal dimension of the condition lives.
  • Structural nervous system support — when the sympathetic baseline is chronically elevated, the column is perpetually contracted. Structural floor-lowering is foundational.
  • Community and genuine connection — real warmth, belonging, and love restore the column from the relational layer.
  • Being of service — purpose beyond personal relief changes the quality of everything.
  • Behavioral rewriting techniques — when vertical alignment is sufficiently present, available for writing new associations in the conditioned response layer.

7. Love as Mechanism

Less struggle. More love.

This is precise, not poetic.

Behavioral rewriting works because a genuinely felt positive emotional state is neurologically incompatible with threat response. The new association that rewrites the old one must be felt in the body — warmth, expansion, genuine positive emotion. Love is not a supplement to the mechanism. Love is what the mechanism runs on.

In triggered moments: the shift from what are they doing to me toward what is happening in me is a movement toward self-compassion. The nervous system in a triggered state is not malfunctioning. It is frightened. Meeting it with warmth changes what is available in the very next moment.

The parts of the system organizing around the condition — the protectors generating reactivity, the vulnerability they protect — are met with curious, compassionate attention. They have been working very hard for a very long time. When they are genuinely heard, they begin to relax.

At the largest scale: healing done in the knowledge that it returns one more fully to others is healing of a different quality. The condition that has most impaired daily life is precisely about being present with other people. The healed sensitivity — attuned, skilled, loving — makes genuine presence possible. That is what this practice is for.

8. The Incubation Principle

Three eggs at the center. Twelve spirals of natural cycle surrounding them. Turtle wisdom overhead. Eyes closed, vision inward.

Eggs do not hatch faster with more effort. They hatch with warmth, stillness, and proper timing. Forcing emergence breaks them.

Slow is not failure. Slow is often the signal that something real is incubating beneath the surface — something that needs the full time it needs.

The most important changes in this practice happen underground — in sleep, in the gradual opening of the listening pier, in the accumulation of moments where sensitivity was met with curiosity rather than fear, in the quiet development of capacities that have no visible metric.

Whenever the impulse to intensify, add, accelerate, or optimize arises — pause. Bring it to the listening pier first. Ask whether it arises from genuine felt readiness or from the old hypervigilant problem-solving mode that knows how to disguise itself as wisdom. Often it is the latter.

9. The Mechanistic Foundation — As Working Hypothesis

The neuroscience is real and useful. It is not the authority.

The best currently available science describes a conditioned threat response that activates faster than conscious regulation can reach — involving the auditory system, threat-detection centers, the autonomic nervous system, and social-threat coding attached to specific people. Running ahead of everything is anticipatory activation — the system pre-mobilizing before any trigger occurs.

This map informs which tools are useful and in what sequence. It is genuinely valuable. And it is not the whole story.

Direct experience — what surfaces in sessions, in dreams, in deep listening, in the body, in guidance received through plant medicine and other channels — holds an authority that no external model fully captures.

Where deep listening contradicts the map, follow the listening. The map is updated accordingly.

The practice makes space for whatever the listening reveals — ancestral material, spiritual dimensions, relational dynamics with roots deeper than any remembered incident, energetic patterns that precede the personal history. It does not insist that the territory conform to the map.

10. The Retreat Provision

There will be times when the greater perspective is temporarily lost. When the surface world is loud, the body depleted, the listening pier overwhelmed, the condition simply painful and the elevated view not accessible. These moments are not failures. They are part of the journey.

The retreat provision is the protected space — always available, always protective, requiring no justification to enter.

  • Restorative embodied practices — restore the listening capacity when it has been overwhelmed. Do not require the elevated view. Create conditions for its return.
  • Practical protective tools — allow participation in daily community life without unnecessary cost while the system recovers.
  • Full pause on active rewiring — no intensification of any kind. Support doubled. This is not failure.
  • Simple in-the-moment tools — always available in any difficult moment, requiring nothing except the breath already present.

Stay in the retreat provision until the listening returns. Until the felt sense says: I can hear again. Then the journey continues from wherever it actually is — not from wherever a schedule says it should be.

Part I

The Container

The container is built and tended daily. It is the conditions the healing and development require — not the work itself, but the ground the work grows from. The daily rhythm, community navigation, and retreat provision together create the environment in which alignment can deepen, sensitivity can develop, and the journey can unfold at its own pace.

I.1 The Daily Rhythm

Sequence governs. Clock does not. The order of the practices matters. The precise time is flexible. When the day disrupts the full sequence, the irreducible minimum is held.

Waking and Grounding

Fixed wake time. The day begins with the body before the mind.

Bare feet on earth — grass, soil, rock, sand — for a few minutes before anything else. Not ritual performance. A genuine return to physical ground. Free electrons, measurable anti-inflammatory effects, immediate parasympathetic shift. The Hamakua Coast is extraordinary medicine for this.

Bright natural light as soon as possible. Outside preferred. Cold water to finish the morning shower — vagal activation from below, the body awakening from the inside out.

On waking, before the shower: one slow breath and one question held without demanding an answer — what is present right now?

Morning Pages or Gratitude Practice

Before consuming any input — before phone, before news — three pages of longhand writing. Stream of consciousness, written at speed, not reread. Whatever is moving in the system gets onto the page and out of the way. Over time this clears the channel to intuitive guidance more reliably than almost anything else.

If three pages isn't available: five genuine sentences of felt gratitude — specific, physically felt rather than performed. Genuine gratitude directly shifts the nervous system's baseline frequency.

TRE — Tension and Trauma Releasing Exercises (10–15 min)

The body's innate neurogenic tremoring — the natural shaking response that humans have largely learned to suppress — allowed to move through the system deliberately each morning. TRE activates the psoas and surrounding structures to produce a self-regulating tremor that discharges stored somatic tension from deep in the nervous system. For a nervous system that has been in chronic defensive posture, this is some of the deepest available somatic medicine. Full instructions in T.3.

Qi-Gong (15–20 min)

Shaking practice followed by slow flowing movement. As the practice deepens, consider moving toward a more complete form — Ba Duan Jin (Eight Brocades) or simple Tai Chi — which develops the felt sense of qi moving through the body more fully. Full instructions in T.3.

Resonance Frequency Breathing (15–20 min)

Six breaths per minute — five-second inhale, five-second exhale — with a genuinely felt positive emotion held in the body simultaneously. The breathing alone is insufficient. The felt emotion is what produces coherence. Supported initially by HeartMath biofeedback to learn what coherence actually feels like; once internalized, the breath practice stands alone. Most portable high-frequency practice in the entire document — available anywhere, anytime.

Sound Practice (when called for)

Vocal toning, chanting, or receptive sound healing as part of the morning when the listening pier calls for it. This is one of the most direct available practices for reorienting the relationship with sound — not by managing the response to external sound, but by developing conscious relationship with sound through the body's own voice. Full entry points in T.4.

The Listening Pier Morning Check-In

After the morning practices, with the matcha: a brief and genuine turn toward the listening pier. Dream journal reviewed. Synchronicities noted. The felt-sense question: what quality does this day have? Not analysis — reception.

On Resistance Training Days

Training moves earlier — it precedes TRE and Qi-Gong, which then serve as post-training somatic processing. Protein immediately after training. Target: three times per week.

On Waterfall Days

The waterfall run is the heart of the day. Zone 2 movement to the waterfall, full cold immersion, the return. Not exercise with a cold finish — a full encounter with the natural world that restores vertical aliveness from the root upward. BDNF elevation, vagal activation, bilateral sensory integration, cortisol rhythm anchoring, grounding. Nothing else in this practice produces this breadth of effect simultaneously. Target: two times per week.

Receptive Nature Time

Unhurried, receptive time in natural surroundings with no agenda except to receive. Not the waterfall run. Simply being present in the living natural world with full sensory openness — sitting under a tree, standing near the ocean, watching light through leaves. One of the most direct practices available for developing the sensitive faculty.

Creative Expression

Making something as a regular element of the day — not as leisure but as a genuine method for developing and expressing the sensitive faculty. Any form: writing, drawing, photography, music, movement. The sensitivity learns what it feels like to create rather than only to receive.

The Evening Sequence

Final meal by 6pm. This anchors the sleep protocol and the overnight descent.

  • Sleep stack (magnesium, glycine, apigenin) 30–60 min before sleep
  • Warm shower — lowers core body temperature
  • Yoga Nidra — every evening without exception. The one practice that should not be skipped.
  • Screens off. Pink noise on. Blackout mask on. Sleep.
Night Waking

Do not check the phone. Put on the sleep-specific Yoga Nidra track immediately. Maintain the theta-alpha state even if sleep does not return. The cortisol-driven frustration loop that compounds night waking is interrupted by this single act.

The Irreducible Minimum

On disrupted days, three things hold the container upright: one embodied morning practice — even five minutes of TRE. The listening pier check-in — even one breath and one question. Yoga Nidra before sleep — even twenty minutes.

The Daily Operating Quality
Resourced
Full engagement. Active work when called for. When a plasticity window is open and the system is simultaneously resourced — maximum presence. This is the highest-value window in the practice.
Frayed
A support day. All embodied practices. No active rewriting, no deliberate exposure, no intensification. Nourishment. Rest. Let the container hold without adding demand.
Overwhelmed
The retreat provision activates. Full stop on active work. Support doubled. Not failure — the practice protecting what has been built.

I.2 Community Navigation

The community is simultaneously the primary trigger environment and the primary relational healing field. These are not contradictions — they are the same living tension being worked through the practice.

Before Any Shared Space

Brief check-in: resourced, frayed, or overwhelmed? On overwhelmed days, a solo meal or absence from a meeting is not avoidance — it is appropriate protection. On frayed days, full protective tools as default. On resourced days, engage lightly or fully as the system allows.

Sound Support

Bone conduction headphones playing barely perceptible pink noise. Leave the ear canals open so speech is heard normally while keeping the thalamic scanning reflex occupied. Volume at the threshold of perception. Earplugs as backup on difficult days.

Post-Context Reset

After any high-load shared context: two to five minutes before the next demand. Physiological sigh, slow walk, cold water on face, or simply standing somewhere quiet. This is physiological action, not analysis.

The Three-Move Sequence — In Any Triggered Moment
1
Physiological — First 5 seconds
One physiological sigh. Double inhale through the nose — two sharp inhales stacking — then one long slow exhale. Mechanically activates the vagal brake. Hardware, not philosophy.
2
Orientation — 10 seconds
Feel the feet on the floor. Feel the weight of the body. One thing visible in the room. This tells the brainstem: I am here. I am not in danger. This is a room.
3
Listening — 10–20 seconds
Instead of contracting, bracing, or escaping — a moment of genuine curiosity about what is happening inside. Not about the sound. Where is it in the body? What shape and quality does it have, before the story starts? This is the beginning of a different relationship with the condition.
If the system is genuinely flooded after these three moves: leave. A regulated exit is always correct. The listening happens in the window before flooding, not after it. Under one minute. Most of it is silence.
The Social Contract

"I'm working through a sound sensitivity condition that significantly affects me in shared spaces. I'm doing structured work on it and expect it to improve meaningfully over time. In the meantime I'll sometimes be wearing headphones at meals or meetings, adjusting where I sit, or stepping out briefly. This is deliberate and health-related, not social. I don't need accommodation — just awareness."

One conversation with core community leadership before active work begins. Done.

I.3 The Retreat Provision

The retreat provision is always available, always protective, requiring no justification to enter. The only question: does the system need this right now?

When to Enter
  • Sleep poor three or more consecutive nights despite practices
  • Body tension and reactivity rising across days rather than settling
  • Active rewriting sessions consistently ending worse than they began
  • Growing dread or avoidance around any core practice
  • Rumination increasing rather than decreasing
  • The practice itself feeling oppressive rather than supportive
  • Resentment toward the practice or the condition
What the Retreat Provision Is
  • Full suspension of active rewriting, plant medicine, and any intensive interventions
  • Embodied practices doubled — longer TRE, longer Yoga Nidra, more receptive nature time
  • Practical protective tools in place for all community contexts. No heroics
  • Biological support maintained — supplements, nourishment, sleep continue regardless
  • Time in nature without agenda. No protocol analysis
Duration

Minimum 48 hours. No maximum. Stay until the felt sense says: I can hear again. First reengagement: short, low-stakes, gentle. If the retreat provision is needed repeatedly: the pace of the larger journey needs to slow. This is information, not failure.

I.4 The Listening Pier in Daily Life

Dream Journal

Pen and paper by the bed. Whatever is present upon waking written before the day begins. Received, not analyzed. Patterns emerge over weeks. The dream intelligence becomes a recognized voice — one of the most reliable sources of guidance in the entire practice. Review periodically for recurring themes and persistent images.

Daily Felt-Sense Check-In

Once each day, briefly: what is actually present right now? Anywhere, any posture. A genuine pause and a sincere question. The answer is not in words — it is in the quality of what arises in the body.

Noticing Synchronicities

When something arrives that feels like signal — note it. The act of noticing and recording trains the attention that makes receiving more possible. No interpretation required immediately. The channel opens in proportion to how faithfully it is listened to.

Energy Reading and Intuitive Development

Begins with noticing what is already arriving: the felt sense of a space before entering it, the quality of an interaction before words are spoken, the pull toward or away from something without rational basis. These are early signals from a faculty that has always been present.

As the capacity develops, its range naturally expands — from personal felt-sense to attunement with others, to broader fields of intelligence including non-physical intelligence. The direction of travel is always toward greater openness, following what the practice reveals. Specific resources in T.9.

Morning Pages as Channel

Over time, morning pages often become more than clearing — they become a channel. Guidance, insight, and intelligence that doesn't feel like ordinary thought begins to arrive through the writing. This is a documented phenomenon in the sustained practice. When it begins to happen, receive it without overanalyzing.

I.5 Relational Practices

The condition has most impaired the capacity for genuine presence with others. The third movement is about returning that capacity — as something developed and enriched, not merely restored.

Authentic Relating and Circling

Structured relational practices that develop the capacity for genuine presence with others — real listening, real contact, real intimacy in a safe container. These directly develop what the condition has most impaired. Available through workshops, online courses, and practice groups.

Nonviolent Communication (NVC)

A practice of genuine listening and genuine expression. Develops empathy — the capacity to sense what is alive in another person — directly aligned with the energy reading development the practice is building. Resource: Nonviolent Communication by Marshall Rosenberg.

Community as Practice Ground

Every shared meal. Every meeting. Every moment of friction. These are the third movement asking to be inhabited — gently, imperfectly, with the tools available. The community is not the obstacle to healing. It is the field in which healing becomes embodied.

Part II

The Arc

What follows is not a roadmap. It is an orientation. The actual arc of this practice will be revealed by the journey itself. It cannot be written in advance. When the territory and this description diverge, follow the territory. The listening pier governs.
First Movement — Descent and Restoration

The journey begins underground. Not with intervention but with genuine descent — into the body, into stillness, into the root layer where the noise of the surface world cannot easily reach.

This movement tends to feel like: building, establishing, settling. The container taking shape. The morning rhythm finding its natural form. Sleep deepening. The body beginning to release chronic bracing. The listening pier opening in its earliest and most basic form.

Structural floor-lowering belongs here — the physiological expression of the descent, before behavioral work asks anything significant of the nervous system above the floor. Plant medicine in its orientation form — without agenda — belongs here.

Signs that this movement is deepening: the morning practices begin to feel like part of the day rather than tasks. Sleep is noticeably different. Something ambient has shifted slightly — hard to name but real.

Second Movement — Alignment and Attunement

When the container is genuinely established, something becomes possible that was not possible before. This movement tends to feel like: opening, deepening, sometimes intense, sometimes extraordinarily spacious.

The listening pier comes alive in ways the first movement only hinted at. Plant medicine shifts from orientation to genuine encounter. The energy reading and intuitive capacities begin to open in ways that feel real rather than aspirational.

The sound practices — toning, chanting, sound healing — begin to transform the relationship with sound itself. Active behavioral rewriting belongs here too — when alignment is present, when the felt sense says the time is right. As a precision instrument, not a schedule.

When the felt sense says the work has become oppressive rather than intense: the retreat provision. When the felt sense says there is more available: follow it.

Third Movement — Embodiment and Service

What has been developed becomes lived. The sensitivity that once fired as alarm now has a place to go — toward genuine attunement, toward service, toward the connection that was always its deepest desire.

The relational practices come into their fullest expression here. The energy reading capacity finds its natural outlet in service to others. The creative expression deepens. The condition may still arise — but its quality changes. From overwhelming reactivity to useful signal. From something that impairs presence to something that informs it.

This movement does not conclude the practice. It is what the practice was always for. The return upstream. The full spiral of the patient year. The hatched egg, finally, in the world.

The arc is real. The journey fills it in.

Part III

The Toolkit

The tools are available when the listening pier says they are called for. They are not the practice. They serve it. Use the Toolkit when something specific is being called for — not before.

Stellate Ganglion Block — DSR Protocol

Structurally lowers the sympathetic baseline by temporarily interrupting overactivated NGF signaling in the stellate ganglion. The effect is a durable reset — not sedation or suppression — that can persist for months to years.

The DSR (Dual Sympathetic Reset) protocol: right stellate ganglion first, left side 24–72 hours later. The right side addresses amygdala reactivity and HPA axis overdrive. The left restores autonomic balance and parasympathetic coordination.

Provider requirements: Anesthesiologist or pain physician experienced in SGB. Ultrasound guidance non-negotiable. Disclose all supplements and substances in advance.

Horner's syndrome — temporary drooping of the eyelid, pupil constriction, mild flushing on the injected side — is normal and expected. It confirms the block reached the stellate ganglion effectively. Resolves within hours.

Post-procedure: rest Day 1. From Day 2, notice ambient life. The effect develops over 1–4 weeks.

Pre-SGB Supplement Pause

Pause 1 week before: krill oil, all omega-3 supplements, NAC, vitamin E. All resume 48 hours after the procedure.

Pause microdosing 7 days before. Space 72+ hours between any psilocybin macrodose and SGB. No alcohol 7 days before.

Continue normally: all other supplements.

Baseline Biological Testing

Before the practice begins in earnest:

  • Comprehensive bloodwork: metabolic panel, CBC, lipids with ApoB, full thyroid with TPO antibodies, hormones (testosterone, DHEA-S, cortisol), inflammation markers (hsCRP, homocysteine), micronutrients (vitamin D, B12, ferritin, zinc, magnesium RBC), blood glucose markers, IGF-1
  • HPA axis profile: salivary cortisol panel across four time points
  • Cellular NAD+ level: actual status before supplementation. Calibrates NMN dosing.

These form the pre-practice biological portrait. Repeat at one year. Trajectory over time is what matters.

Legal Note

Psilocybin and MDMA are Schedule I substances in the United States. Hawaii has no state-level decriminalization. Know your legal context. Hard contraindications: personal or family history of psychosis, schizophrenia, or bipolar I; current SSRIs, SNRIs, pharmaceutical lithium, or MAOIs; heart conditions; pregnancy.

Psilocybin — Orientation Sessions

Dose: 1.5–2.0g. Intention: none. Arrive openly. No agenda beyond familiarity — learning how this medicine feels before it is ever asked to work.

Setting: private, sacred if possible. Genuinely safe and unhurried. Sitter: a trusted, calm witness — not a guide.

Integration (Days 1–3): rest. Journal if the impulse arises. Light movement from Day 2. Full supplement stack resumes.

Psilocybin — Therapeutic Sessions

Dose: 2.0–3.5g, calibrating from experience. Intention: 1–3 sentences, honest and specific, offered with open hands.

Questions to offer the medicine: What is this sensitivity for? What wants to emerge? What is being asked of me? What is ready to be released?

Microdosing — Stamets Stack

Schedule: 4 days on (Mon–Thu), 3 days off. Dose: 0.1–0.15g psilocybin + 100–200mg niacin 30 minutes after. Timing: morning with or after food.

Sub-perceptual neuroplasticity enhancement. Pause 1 week before any macrodose session. Pause 7 days before SGB. Resume Day 4–5 after macrodose or MDMA integration.

MDMA

Dose: 80–120mg. First session toward lower end. Minimum interval: 6 weeks. Maximum: two sessions in any 12-month period. A trusted sitter is strongly recommended.

MDMA targets the heart layer — where the interpersonal dimension of the condition lives, where specific people's sounds carry relational charge beyond the acoustic trigger.

Preparation: stop NAC 3 days before. Skip ashwagandha on session day. No 5-HTP or tryptophan 24 hours before. Pause microdosing 1 week before. No alcohol 7 days before.

Integration: Day 1 rest (MDMA disrupts REM 1–3 nights — maintain Yoga Nidra). Days 3–5: EMDR on specific interpersonal memories that arose. 5-HTP 100mg + EGCG 400mg for 3 days from 24 hours after. Resume microdosing Day 4–5.

TRE Full Instructions

Lie on back, soles of feet together, knees falling outward. Allow the inner thigh muscles to begin tremoring naturally — this typically takes 2–5 minutes of holding the position. Once tremoring begins, allow it to spread and move through the body without directing or suppressing it. Stay curious and present. 10–15 minutes.

To intensify: slowly raise the hips off the floor. Lower if tremoring becomes overwhelming. The body regulates intensity when allowed to.

Come to stillness. Legs flat, arms at sides. Notice the body for 2–3 minutes before moving.

On days of illness: 5 minutes only or gentle shaking from standing. If tremoring moves into strong emotion: allow it, stay with the breath, let it complete.

Over time TRE becomes available in other positions — standing, sitting — as the body learns the tremoring mechanism.

Somatic Release — Simplified (7–10 min)

Constructive rest (5 min): lie on back, knees bent, feet flat, hands on lower belly. Allow lower back to release toward floor. Breathe into the hands. Jaw released, eyes soft.

Diaphragmatic reset (2–3 min): inhale 4 counts into belly, pause 2 counts, exhale 6 counts. Allow jaw and shoulders to release on each exhale.

Somatic Release — Full (20–25 min, before active sessions)

Constructive rest (5 min) → Psoas release (5 min — one knee drops slowly outward, hold 2 min each side) → Hip circles (3 min each direction and figure-eights) → Shoulder and neck release (3 min — slow semicircles forward only, shrugs, jaw drops) → Diaphragmatic reset with audible release (3 min) → Integration in standing (2 min).

Do not force sharp pain. Psoas release should feel like release, not stretch.

Qi-Gong Protocol

Shaking (3–5 min): feet shoulder-width, knees slightly bent. Gentle rhythmic bouncing — allowing the body to vibrate softly. Arms loose. Jaw released. Allow the body to shake rather than making it shake.

Slow flowing (10–15 min): Eight Brocades (Ba Duan Jin) or consistent Tai Chi warm-up sequence. Slow, intentional movement with coordinated breath. Exhale on exertion, inhale on opening.

Breathwork

Wim Hof Method: 30–40 deep full inhalations followed by full releases (not forced exhales). After the final release, hold the breath out as long as comfortable. Then one full recovery breath held 15 seconds. Three rounds. Builds autonomic resilience and reduces baseline inflammation.

Holotropic-style breathing: sustained connected breathing at elevated pace for 20–45 minutes, lying down with evocative music. Opens access to non-ordinary states. Best done with a trained facilitator initially. Approach with same respect and intention as plant medicine.

This category addresses the condition from its most direct angle: developing a new relationship with sound through the body itself. Not by managing the response to external sound — by developing conscious relationship with sound from the inside out.

Vocal Toning

Sustained single tones — vowels or sacred syllables — held and allowed to resonate through the body. Begin with "A" (as in "ah"): sit comfortably, full breath, let the sound emerge and sustain for 10–20 seconds. Feel where it resonates. Move through E, I, O, U with the same quality of attention.

The seed mantras of the chakra system (LAM, VAM, RAM, YAM, HAM, OM) work through the same principle: sustained vibration experienced from the inside. 5–10 minutes of daily toning begins to transform the relationship with sound at a fundamental level.

Chanting

Longer-form sustained vocal practice — mantras repeated for 10–20 minutes, kirtan, Gregorian chant, or devotional singing in any resonant tradition. The specific tradition matters less than the sustained quality of attention and the physical experience of vibration moving through the body.

Sound Healing as Receiver

Sound baths using singing bowls (crystal or Tibetan), gongs, tuning forks, or other vibrational instruments. Lying down, eyes closed, fully receptive. The body responding to frequencies rather than defending against them. For someone whose condition has been about a dysregulated response to sound, this is retraining at the most fundamental level.

A regular sound bath practice — monthly or more — belongs in this practice when accessible. A personal quality singing bowl is a lifetime tool worth acquiring.

Safe and Sound Protocol (SSP)

A 5-hour auditory intervention using music processed to enhance the human vocal frequency range. Retrains the neural circuits governing auditory safety detection — specifically the stapedius muscle's ability to dampen threat-associated frequencies. Operates upstream of the conditioned response at the auditory hardware level.

Platform: Unyte-iLs (licensed provider required for primary course). 30–60 minutes per session over 1–2 weeks. Lie down, headphones only, no multitasking. Do not run active rewriting sessions on SSP days.

After the primary course: 20–30 minutes daily of music in the human vocal frequency range — acoustic folk, unprocessed vocal harmonies, classical violin — in an alert, receptive state. This ongoing practice maintains and deepens the auditory safety training. It is also, simply, a beautiful daily practice.

These are precision instruments. Employed when vertical alignment is sufficiently present and the listening pier confirms readiness. Not before. Not on a schedule.

Counterconditioning — The Entry Condition

Two non-negotiable conditions before any rewriting session: subjective distress level 0–2 (0–10 scale) and body tension below 4/10. Reached through resonance frequency breathing for 3–5 minutes. If not achievable in 5 minutes: frayed day. No session.

Building the Resource Library

Positive resource library (15–20 items): music, nature sounds, or sensory experiences that reliably produce genuinely felt positive emotion in the body. Test each — does it produce an actual physical shift? If not, it does not qualify. Draw from personal history.

Trigger sound library: organized by distress level. FOAMS database (Zenodo DOI: 10.5281/zenodo.4813431) — 32 validated files across 8 sound classes. Freesound.org and yt-dlp for additional sourcing. Self-recording for person-specific sounds.

Basic Technique

  1. Full somatic release sequence
  2. Resonance frequency breathing 3–5 min. Confirm entry condition met.
  3. Neuropod if using — start 2–3 min before session
  4. Begin positive resource. 2–3 min. Wait for genuinely felt positive state.
  5. Introduce trigger sound at low volume. Positive resource dominant. Hold 2–4 min. Positive state must remain felt throughout.
  6. Remove trigger. Return to positive resource. Let state rebuild fully — minimum 2 min.
  7. Repeat 4–6 times. Stop if distress rises above 4 or positive state cannot be recovered.
  8. End on positive resource only. Final 2 minutes.
  9. Brief note: distress before, after, one observation.

Session frequency when using: 2–3 per week at most. Quality of entry matters more than frequency.

The Pulse Principle

72 hours after a plant medicine session: highest-value rewriting window of the cycle. BDNF elevated. Default mode network grip loosened. Day 1 is always rest. Days 2–3 are the most productive rewriting time.

EMDR

For the interpersonal layer — memories where the trigger is not just acoustic but relational. Use a bilateral audio app (alternating left-right tones at 1 Hz). Headphones required.

Target one specific memory. Rate distress 0–10. Bring fully to mind. Start bilateral audio. Hold memory while tones run in 30-second sets. Notice what shifts. Check in after each set. Continue if distress is reducing. Close and ground if stable or rising after 3–4 sets. When distress reaches 0–2: install a brief positive cognition. Grounding: 5 breaths, 5 things visible, feet on floor.

Working with a trained EMDR therapist is preferable for high-charge memories.

IFS — Internal Family Systems

For the parts of the system organizing around the condition — met with curious, compassionate attention. Not correction. Contact.

Entry: sit quietly. Notice where the condition lives in the body. Ask that sensation: what are you protecting? What are you afraid would happen if you weren't here? Listen without demanding answers.

The first session producing genuine contact with a protective part — not resolution, contact — is a significant step. Resource: No Bad Parts (Richard Schwartz).

Propranolol-Assisted Reconsolidation

When a conditioned response is reactivated, it briefly becomes labile and can be modified. Propranolol impairs re-storage of the emotional component while preserving the factual memory. Requires prescription. 10–20mg per session, 60–90 minutes before a rewriting session.

Target distress during session: 5–7. Log each session including the 24-hour re-rating — the most meaningful data point. Not within 48 hours of plant medicine. Not with asthma, bradycardia, or hypotension.

ACT and Anti-Rumination

ACT defusion: when trigger-related thoughts arise, prefix silently — "I notice I am having the thought that..." Creates distance without suppression. The Happiness Trap by Russ Harris.

Anti-rumination: when the loop is recognized — pattern interrupt immediately (stand up, change room, cold water, physiological sigh). Then ACT defusion. For persistent loops with something real: 5–10 minutes, pen and paper, write it out, then close the notebook.

These tools develop or amplify biological capacities that the embodied practices are also building. They are transitional support — each teaches the body a capacity, and steps back as that capacity is internalized.

HRV Biofeedback — HeartMath Inner Balance

Used to learn the felt sense of cardiac coherence. Once internalized, resonance frequency breathing is the primary practice. Use daily for 4–6 weeks. Always Free Session mode — never the guided programs. Always through headphones, never speaker. Compatible with Elite HRV app as alternative.

HRV Tracking

To establish the biological baseline. Use for 6–8 weeks to understand starting conditions, then transition to periodic confirmation as felt sense becomes primary.

  • Option A: Wearable with sleep tracking — continuous data. Switch if it disrupts sleep.
  • Option B: Morning chest strap — Polar H10, 5 min each morning.
  • Option C: Weekly spot-check — fingertip pulse oximeter. Sufficient for trend tracking.

tVNS — Neuropod / Nurosym

Clips to the tragus of the left ear. Gentle electrical pulses to the auricular branch of the vagus nerve. Amplifies vagal tone during rewriting sessions. A precision tool, not a session requirement — use when it genuinely enhances the session without distraction. The goal is to not need it. Not during driving. Not during plant medicine sessions.

tDCS — ActivaDose or equivalent

Upregulates excitability of the left dorsolateral prefrontal cortex. Applied 20 minutes before a rewriting session. Anodal: left dlPFC (position F3). Reference: right supraorbital. Settings: 1.5–2.0 mA. Hold as a precision tool for periods of intensive rewriting work. Not within 5 hours of sleep. Not within 24 hours of plant medicine. Not during retreat provision.

Tracking and Measurement

The primary instrument is felt sense. Data confirms or questions it. Any tracking that has become a burden is simplified or dropped.

  • Weekly: re-rate full trigger hierarchy. One sentence capturing the week's quality. Brief session note immediately after each rewriting session.
  • Monthly: A-MISO-S questionnaire (baseline 18). HRV trend direction.
  • Quarterly: avoidance inventory — what situations are still being avoided or heavily managed? The shrinking of this list is the most meaningful functional outcome.
  • Biological testing: full bloodwork, salivary cortisol, and cellular NAD+ before practice begins. Repeat at one year with added Omega-3 Index (OmegaQuant) and epigenetic age test.

Grounding and Earthing

Bare feet on earth daily — grass, soil, rock, sand. Even 5 minutes. Free electrons from the earth's surface have measurable anti-inflammatory effects. The felt effect is immediate. The Hamakua Coast offers extraordinary grounding opportunities.

Authentic Relating and Circling

Structured relational practices that develop the capacity for genuine presence with others — real listening, real contact, real intimacy in a safe container. Available through workshops, online courses, and practice groups. Among the most powerful available supports for the third movement.

Nonviolent Communication (NVC)

A practice of genuine listening and genuine expression. Develops empathy — the capacity to sense what is alive in another person — directly aligned with the energy reading development the practice is building. Core resource: Nonviolent Communication by Marshall Rosenberg.

Energy Medicine — Reiki

Learning to consciously sense, move, and work with the body's energy field, received through a lineage with a qualified teacher. Reiki develops through the hands, in contact with others. It is inherently a service practice. It directly develops the energy reading capacity in a way that purely receptive practices cannot. Receiving Reiki regularly from a practitioner is available regardless of whether one is learning to offer it — both are valuable.

These resources are held lightly — as starting points that the listening pier will refine over time. What is named here is available. What the practice actually calls for will be revealed by the journey.

Structured Channeling and Intuitive Communication

Making the Connection — Suzanne Giezman. The primary structured container for developing channeling and intuitive communication. A real curriculum with a teacher to return to consistently.

Community Practice

Practicing in small, trusted settings — held in curiosity, playfulness, and genuine service to others. Beginning intimate and low-stakes. A few trusted community members. The MDMA-received encouragement to practice with community was specific and worth honoring.

Expanded Field Attunement

Bashar (Darryl Anka): transmissions and meditations for expanding attunement toward ET intelligence and non-physical guidance. Opens the listening pier toward frequencies and dimensions body-focused practices alone cannot reach.

Elevated Emotion and Quantum Field

Dr. Joe Dispenza: structured practices for deliberately generating and sustaining elevated emotional and energetic states as a primary mode of being. Directly supports the high vibrational frequency core of this practice.

Animal and Nature Communication

Practices for attunement with animals and the natural world — a gentle, accessible entry into non-verbal, non-physical communication. The Hamakua Coast is a powerful environment for this.

The General Principle

The development of intuitive capacity is nonlinear. It opens in unexpected moments, in states of genuine rest and receptivity rather than effortful practice. The same conditions that support healing — genuine stillness, reduced sympathetic activation, open awareness — are the conditions that support the opening of intuitive capacity.

They are the same work.

Legal and Safety Note

Psilocybin and MDMA are Schedule I substances in the United States. Hawaii has no state-level decriminalization. Know your legal context before proceeding.

Hard contraindications for psilocybin and MDMA: personal or family history of psychosis, schizophrenia, or bipolar I; current SSRIs, SNRIs, pharmaceutical lithium, or MAOIs; heart conditions; pregnancy.

Stellate Ganglion Block requires an experienced physician and full medical history review. Insist on ultrasound guidance. Nothing in this document is medical advice.

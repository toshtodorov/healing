The Three-Move Sequence — Any Triggered Moment

1Physiological — 5 sec. Double inhale through nose → one long slow exhale. Vagal brake activated.
2Orientation — 10 sec. Feet on floor. Body weight. One visible thing.
3Listening — 10–20 sec. Where is this in the body? What shape before the story starts? Curiosity about the interior, not the source.
If flooded after three moves: leave. Regulated exit is always correct.

The Social Contract

"I'm working through a sound sensitivity condition that significantly affects me in shared spaces. I'm doing structured work on it. In the meantime I'll sometimes be wearing headphones, adjusting where I sit, or stepping out briefly. This is deliberate and health-related, not social."

One conversation with core community leadership before active work begins.

Retreat Provision

When to Stop and Reset

Always available. No justification required. Stay until the felt sense says: I can hear again.

Enter when three or more present — or any single one severe
  • Sleep poor 3+ consecutive nights
  • Reactivity rising across days rather than settling
  • Active rewriting sessions consistently ending worse than they began
  • Growing dread or avoidance around any core practice
  • Rumination increasing rather than decreasing
  • The practice itself feels oppressive
  • Resentment toward the practice or the condition
Active work: full suspension — no rewriting, no plant medicine, no intensive interventions
Embodied practices: doubled — longer TRE, longer Yoga Nidra, more receptive nature time
Substrate: supplements, nourishment, sleep continue unchanged
Duration: minimum 48 hours, no maximum

The Practice — Healing, Alignment & Awakening Sensitivity

The living foundation from which The Practice grows. Read this before The Practice. Return to it whenever The Practice starts to feel like something other than what it is. Written April 2026.

Five Orientations — Everything in Service of This
  • Alignment
  • Healthy sensitivity attunement
  • Love
  • Being of service
  • Raising to high vibrational frequency
The compass. When any part of The Practice feels wrong — too heavy, too mechanical, too urgent — return to these five and ask: is what I am doing genuinely in service of this?
2

The Condition as Messenger

The sound sensitivity condition is real, sometimes painful, and for a long time has felt like something to be defeated. It is not something to be defeated. It is a messenger pointing toward a sensitivity that needed to be understood, honored, and brought into alignment with its true purpose.

The hypersensitivity that makes certain sounds overwhelming is the same sensitivity that, developed and aligned, becomes the capacity for deep attunement — to people, to subtle energy, to guidance, to the living intelligence that moves through all things.

The healing is not the silencing of that signal. It is the honoring of what it was pointing at. When alignment deepens, the signal naturally quiets — because it has been heard.
3

The Governing Philosophy

The healing intelligence is already present. This practice creates the conditions in which healing that is already seeking to happen can move through.

This is not war. This is restoration.

The organizing question is not how do we reduce reactivity? — it is what restores alignment and develops the capacity to hold sensitivity as a gift rather than a wound?

4

The Three Living Movements

Not a ladder — a spiral. Three movements that breathe throughout the entire journey.

I

Descent & Restoration

Go underground before acting. Quiet the noise enough that deeper signals can be heard. Lower the floor. Build genuine safety. This is not preparation for healing — it is the beginning of it.

II

Alignment & Attunement

When genuine stillness is produced, something reorients — not through effort. The overflowing source feeds the whole vertical system naturally. Where conscious attunement is developed.

III

Embodiment & Service

What has been developed is brought back into daily life. The sensitivity oriented outward as gift. The community is simultaneously the primary trigger environment and the primary field of practice.

5

Developing felt-sense, deep listening, intuitive attunement, energy reading, and conscious connection with guidance beyond the physical is the foundational skill that everything else serves.

The organizing question: not which intervention addresses which symptom — but what restores and deepens vertical flow?

Nourishment — food as care for the instrument; physical foundation of the column's clarity
Embodied movement and somatic practice — releasing chronic tension, restoring the body as open channel

Behavioral rewriting — when alignment is present, precision technique for writing new associations
7

Love as Mechanism

Less struggle. More love. This is precise, not poetic.

Behavioral rewriting works because a genuinely felt positive emotional state is neurologically incompatible with threat response. Love is what the mechanism runs on.

In triggered moments: the shift from what are they doing to me toward what is happening in me is a movement toward self-compassion. The nervous system in a triggered state is not malfunctioning. It is frightened.

8

The Incubation Principle

Eggs do not hatch faster with more effort — they hatch with warmth, stillness, and proper timing. Slow is not failure.

When the impulse to intensify, add, or accelerate arises — pause. Bring it to the listening pier first. Ask whether it arises from genuine felt readiness or from the old hypervigilant problem-solving mode.
9

The Mechanistic Foundation — As Working Hypothesis

The neuroscience is real and useful. It is not the authority. The science describes a conditioned threat response activating faster than conscious regulation can reach — auditory system, threat-detection centers, autonomic nervous system, social-threat coding, anticipatory activation.

Where deep listening contradicts the map, follow the listening. The map is updated accordingly.

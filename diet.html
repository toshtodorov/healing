Daily Structure

The 4-Window Eating Schedule

Built around the RB community schedule. Timing is as important as content — each window has a mechanistic role. Subject to adjustment as the structure is trialed.

Training Days

Meal 1 — Post-Training
Protein smoothie immediately after training. Supplement stack taken here (NMN sublingual 5 min before). Matcha with or after.
11:30am

Light-to-moderate second meal. Keep protein real, not heavy. Zinc here (Week 3+).

Aerobic / Waterfall Days

11:30am

Post-waterfall, substantial. Full morning supplement stack taken here. NMN sublingual 5 min before eating.
2:45pm
Main Meal

4:15pm optional
Closer

Fasting Window Summary
Training days~15–16h fast (4:15pm → 6:30am next day). Autophagy and AMPK signaling in the morning window before training.
Aerobic days~19h+ fast (4:15pm → 11:30am next day). The longest and most powerful cellular-clearing window of the week. Do not shorten.
Evening cutoffHard stop at 4:15pm. Late calories delay sleep onset, suppress melatonin, compress deep sleep, and partially dissolve the CC/EMDR associations formed during the day. The evening cutoff protects every structural change the protocol produced.
If underfueledAdd calories first to the main meal — more quinoa, sweet potato, lentils, tofu, or tempeh. Use the 4:15pm closer as a genuine last resort, not a habit.
Sleep signalIf sleep worsens after starting the closer, remove the closer first before adjusting anything else.
Why early eating specifically here: CC and EMDR produce new associations that consolidate during slow-wave sleep. An early last meal means glucose and insulin are settled before sleep onset, core body temperature drops on schedule, and overnight cortisol is not elevated by late digestion. The protocol's behavioral work is only as permanent as the sleep that follows it.

Daily Anchors

Non-Negotiable Daily Foods

These aren't nice-to-haves. Each fills a mechanism the supplement stack does not fully cover. Missing any of these daily is a protocol gap.

Berries
Daily · 100–150g blueberries or blackberries
Pterostilbene + anthocyanins → BDNF. Frozen = fresh for this purpose. Strawberries weekly for fisetin (senolytic).
Ground Flax
Daily · 1–2 tbsp
ALA + lignans (anti-inflammatory). Must be ground — whole seeds pass through undigested. Dissolves invisibly in smoothies.
Wheat Germ
Daily · 1–2 tbsp
Highest spermidine per gram of any food. Triggers autophagy independently of the fasting window. Add to smoothies or any bowl.
Walnuts
Daily · small handful (~30g)
ALA + ellagitannins → urolithin A (mitophagy, distinct from NMN pathway). Only nut with meaningful omega-3 content.
Mushrooms
Daily · any variety
Ergothioneine — most strongly linked to longevity in large-scale blood analysis. Heat-stable. Oyster (grow station), shiitake, any variety contributes.
Leafy Greens
Daily · substantial volume
Nitrates → nitric oxide (cerebrovascular), folate, lutein. Volume matters — not a garnish, a foundation. Arugula highest nitrates.
Capers
Daily · 1 tbsp on anything
Highest quercetin per gram of any food (~20mg/tbsp). Senolytic. The easiest high-value daily add. Goes in everything.
Fermented Food
Daily · rotate sources
Kimchi, sauerkraut, miso, natto. Different strains feed different bacterial populations. Rotation matters more than volume. Natto replaces K2 supplement (see below).
Lecithin Granules
Daily · 1 tbsp in Meal 1 smoothie
Choline ~375mg as phosphatidylcholine. Most significant micronutrient gap in a plant-forward protocol — acetylcholine synthesis, membrane integrity, methylation. Not in the supplement stack at this level without this.
Pumpkin Seeds
Daily · 30g
~150mg elemental magnesium (reduces Mg Glycinate load) + ~7mg zinc. Zinc is absent from the supplement stack. Essential for BDNF synthesis, testosterone, glutathione peroxidase.
Brazil Nuts
Daily · 2–3 nuts only
100–200mcg selenium. Glutathione peroxidase activity, thyroid T4→T3, neuroprotection. Hard ceiling: never exceed 3 nuts. Upper tolerable intake is 400mcg/day.
Tart Cherry
Evening · 1 tbsp in warm water
Post-melatonin-taper: only significant dietary melatonin source. Anthocyanins + quercetin. Exercise recovery support. Part of the bedtime wind-down sequence.
Supplement Offsets from Food — What You Can Skip
Natto 10g dailySkip K2 MK-7 supplement. 10g natto = 100mcg K2 — exactly the supplement dose. D3 still taken regardless.
Artichoke (1 head)Skip apigenin supplement — 1 head ≈ 70mg (above the 50mg dose). Also provides cynarin (liver support) and inulin (prebiotic).
Celery seed 7g in cookingSkip apigenin supplement — 7g ≈ 50mg apigenin. Used consistently, the apigenin supplement becomes redundant.
UV-exposed shiitake 100gReduce D3 by 1,000–2,000 IU. Gill-side up, midday Hawaii sun, 2–4 hours. D3 is heat-stable after UV generation.
Oily fish ~100gSkip both krill oil and algae oil that day. On mackerel specifically: also skip or halve phosphatidylserine (~480mg PS per 100g mackerel).
Fresh Lion's Mane 100–150gHalve the supplement dose on harvest days. Fresh provides hericenones and erinacines but at 1/10–1/15 the concentration of dual-extracted.
Pumpkin seeds 30g + dark choc 30g + spinach 1 cup~370mg combined elemental Mg — reduce Mg Glycinate proportionally. Mg L-Threonate (brain form) is not reducible from food.
Priority Foods

What to Prioritize and Why

Organized by mechanism. Priority border = highest-leverage for this specific protocol. Each has a mechanistic reason to be here — not general wellness association.

Neuroplasticity & BDNF
Blueberries & Blackberries
100–150g daily — frozen equivalent to fresh
Pterostilbene and anthocyanins upregulate BDNF via the sirtuin pathway — the same mechanism Phase 5 resveratrol uses. Before resveratrol begins, berries prime this pathway. After it begins, they remain complementary.
Dark Chocolate 85%+
20–30g after main meal or with 4:15pm closer
Flavanols and epicatechin improve cerebrovascular function and BDNF expression. Provides ~64mg elemental Mg per 30g, partially reducing the Mg Glycinate supplemental requirement. Theobromine: mild methylxanthine. 85%+ threshold matters.
Walnuts
30g daily
ALA (~2.5g per 30g) plus ellagitannins converted by gut bacteria to urolithin A — mitochondrial renewal pathway distinct from NMN. Only nut with meaningful omega-3 content.
Ground Flaxseed
1–2 tbsp daily, ground
Highest plant ALA source. Lignans with anti-inflammatory properties, soluble fiber. Must be ground — whole seeds pass through undigested. Combined with walnuts, ALA substrate is consistent on non-fish days.
Cellular Renewal & Spermidine
Wheat Germ
1–2 tbsp daily in smoothie or bowl
Highest spermidine concentration per gram of any food. Triggers autophagy independently of the fasting window — cellular clearing between plasticity events. Particularly important during active neuroplasticity phases.
Tempeh
2–3× per week at main meal
High spermidine + complete protein + fermented probiotic culture distinct from kimchi and sauerkraut. Fermentation makes it more digestible. More bioavailable spermidine than most plant sources. Sauté or crumble into bowls.
Pomegranate
80–100g arils or 30ml concentrate daily
Ellagitannins → urolithin A via gut bacteria. Mitochondrial cleanup pathway not covered by any supplement in the stack. Pomegranate concentrate in mini fridge for daily use without preparation. Complementary to walnuts.
Mushrooms (Oyster, Shiitake)
Daily — any variety, grow station priority
Ergothioneine — most strongly linked to reduced disease risk in large-scale blood analysis. Heat-stable. Oyster mushrooms from the grow station provide ongoing access. UV-exposed shiitake provides D3 on sun days.
Gut-Brain Axis
Fermented Rotation
Daily — rotate: natto, kimchi, sauerkraut, miso
Diversifies the microbiome beyond L. reuteri — GABA substrate, serotonin precursor environment, short-chain fatty acids for neuroinflammation baseline. Different fermented foods feed different bacterial populations. Rotating matters more than volume.
Natto
10g daily — thaw overnight, stir into rice or miso
10g = ~100mcg K2 MK-7 (skip supplement on natto days). Also: spermidine contribution and Bacillus subtilis var. natto strain not found in other fermented foods. Store frozen. 10g is small enough to be invisible in food.
Legumes (Lentils, Beans)
Daily or near-daily
Lentils: 179mcg folate per 100g cooked (highest plant source). White beans: 107mg PS per 100g (partial PS supplement offset). Cooled legumes provide resistant starch. Black beans, chickpeas, edamame all contribute choline and complete amino acid coverage.
Edamame
Regularly — shelled, any preparation
Complete protein, choline (~63mg per 100g, complements lecithin granules), trace NMN contribution. Good Meal 2 option on lighter days.
Vitamins, Minerals & Protocol Gaps
Lecithin Granules
1 tbsp daily in Meal 1 smoothie
Choline ~375mg as phosphatidylcholine. Most significant micronutrient gap in the current protocol on a plant-forward diet. Acetylcholine synthesis, neuronal membrane integrity, one-carbon methylation. Not in the supplement stack — this is it.
Pumpkin Seeds (Pepitas)
30g daily — in smoothie, bowl, or as snack
~150mg elemental Mg (reduces Mg Glycinate requirement) and ~7mg zinc. Zinc is absent from the supplement stack and essential for BDNF synthesis, testosterone, and glutathione peroxidase. Most practical plant zinc source here.
Brazil Nuts
2–3 nuts daily — hard ceiling, not more
100–200mcg selenium per 2–3 nuts. Selenium drives glutathione peroxidase (the enzyme NAC serves), thyroid T4→T3 conversion, and neuroprotection. Not in the supplement stack. Do not combine with selenomethionine supplement on the same day — selenium toxicity margin is narrow.
Nutritional Yeast
2 tbsp daily in soups, dressings, smoothies
B-complex broadly (B1–B6, folate) and fortified B12. Reduces the B-complex supplement gap. Note: methylated forms (methylfolate, methylcobalamin) remain supplement-dependent — nutritional yeast fills volume but does not replace the methylated supplement.
Nitric Oxide & Cerebrovascular
Beets + Arugula
Beets 150–200g roasted 3×/wk · Arugula daily when available
Dietary nitrates → nitric oxide. Improved cerebral blood flow documented in acute cognitive performance trials. The same vascular effect waterfall runs and cold immersion produce via exercise is supported from below by dietary nitrates. Beets: roast in bulk, keeps 5 days. Arugula: highest nitrate concentration of any common leafy green (~480mg/100g).
Brassicas
3–4× per week — chop 40 min before cooking
Sulforaphane via myrosinase activation. Potent Nrf2 activator. Preparation is non-negotiable: chop and wait 40 minutes before heat. If time-constrained, add mustard seed powder to cooked brassicas to restore yield. Broccoli sprouts raw require no cooking and are accessible.
Oily fish (optional, 1–2× per week): Sardines, mackerel, salmon — ~1500–2000mg EPA+DHA per 100g. The protocol is plant-forward; supplements carry the omega-3 load daily. Fish is used when it is the practical or highest-leverage option for EPA+DHA loading. On fish days: skip both krill and algae oil. On mackerel days specifically: also skip or halve phosphatidylserine (~480mg PS per 100g mackerel covers the 300mg dose).
Protocol Fit

Preparation Rules and Operating Logic

These rules directly affect bioavailability or mechanistic outcomes. They are not optional refinements.

Preparation Rules That Matter
BrassicasChop and wait 40 minutes before cooking. Chopping ruptures cells and allows myrosinase to produce sulforaphane. Heat destroys myrosinase if applied immediately. If cooking now is unavoidable: add mustard seed powder to the cooked brassica — it provides exogenous myrosinase and restores sulforaphane yield. Raw broccoli sprouts (~30g) require no preparation and skip the supplement that day.
GarlicCrush or chop and wait 10 minutes before cooking. Allicin forms from alliin + alliinase — heat destroys alliinase. The 10-minute wait locks in the conversion. Add to food at the end of cooking rather than the beginning when possible.
Sulforaphane supplementSkip on days eating raw broccoli sprouts (~30g) or broccoli that was properly chopped 40 min before cooking. The food provides the mechanism. Skip if on a Jarrow BroccoMax or equivalent with active myrosinase.
FlaxseedGround only — whole seeds pass through undigested. Pre-ground or grind in batches. Store in refrigerator after grinding; ground flax oxidizes quickly at room temperature.
Apple Cider Vinegar1 tbsp before the 2:45pm main meal — the carb-heaviest meal of the day. Not on an empty stomach. Meal 1 or 2 already consumed by this point, which satisfies that condition.
Food-Based Strategy by Category
LegumesDaily or near-daily. Cooled beans and lentils are mechanistically superior — resistant starch content is highest when cooked and cooled. Cold bean bowls are not a compromise; they are the preferred form for gut microbiome purposes.
Tempeh2–3× per week in the main meal rotation. Provides spermidine, complete protein, and a fermented probiotic culture distinct from other fermented foods in the rotation. ACV applies regardless of whether tempeh is in the meal.
Fermented rotationRotate between natto, kimchi, sauerkraut, miso across the week. Defaulting to one source only feeds one bacterial population. Natto delivers the most mechanistic density per gram (K2, spermidine, Bacillus strain) — 10g is small enough to be imperceptible in rice or soup.
Spice baseDaily: turmeric with black pepper + fat (curcumin bioavailability <1% without piperine and fat), ginger, garlic, oregano. The spice parcel at the main meal handles curcumin delivery in a concentrated dose. The broader spice base is a daily background throughout cooking.

Daily Drinks

Matcha & Green Tea Protocol

Matcha — with Meal 1
TimingWith or just after Meal 1. Training days: ~6:30–7am. Aerobic days: ~11:30am.
What it provides300–400mg L-theanine per cup — more than any supplement capsule provides. EGCG (anti-inflammatory, neuroprotective). Moderate caffeine paired with L-theanine for calm focused alertness. The L-theanine is present at protocol-relevant dose from this single daily cup.
Iron interactionSpace at least 1 hour from iron-rich foods in the same meal. EGCG inhibits non-heme iron absorption. Relevant when lentils, spinach, or tempeh are in Meal 1 — time matcha after, not during.
Green Tea — Afternoon Stand-Alone
Timing~6:00–6:30pm — 2–3 hours before target sleep onset (~9–9:30pm). No food in this window under the new schedule, which removes the iron interaction concern entirely.
Which teaKukicha (twig tea) or sencha. Kukicha is particularly low-caffeine with a pleasant roasted quality — preferred when sleep is a priority. Sencha provides more EGCG.
FunctionReplaces any temptation toward late caffeine. Provides trace L-theanine and ongoing EGCG supply. A ritual-quality standalone drink that fits between the 4:15pm closer and the bedtime wind-down sequence.
